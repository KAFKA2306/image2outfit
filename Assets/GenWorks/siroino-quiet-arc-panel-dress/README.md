# Quiet Arc Panel Dress

Canonical product workspace for `siroino-quiet-arc-panel-dress` from Issue #677.

The accepted construction is a closed knee-length panel dress with a compact stand collar, continuous shell, center walking vent, and exactly two structural crescent side arches. Each arch has separate outer and inner rails, a facing, and a real bounded negative-space window. The rejected hoodie, detached balloon sleeves, ribbons, leg straps, cape, and translucent waterfall identity are not part of this product.

The ProductManifest records the missing GitHub-visible source-board persistence as an unverified reference gate. Blender/FBX/render evidence is produced by the canonical product build and pose-render paths. Unity, Modular Avatar, NDMF, and VRChat runtime status remain unverified until directly executed.
